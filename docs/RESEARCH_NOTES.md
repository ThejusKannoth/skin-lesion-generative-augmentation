Because the split sizes differ (992 vs 1,002 test images) and the architectures differ, neither an improvement nor a decline can be causally attributed to augmentation.

Unless the GAN input folders were already training-only, the generators may have seen images that later entered validation/test. Also verify no image ID or duplicate/near-duplicate crosses partitions.
